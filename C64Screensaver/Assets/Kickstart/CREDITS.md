# Historical Kickstart screen artwork

These are unmodified historical screen captures, not ROM files or executable
Amiga system software. They retain their original artwork and on-screen credits.
The repository's MIT license covers our implementation, not these third-party
screen graphics; no ownership or new license to that artwork is claimed here.

- kickstart-13.gif: Kickstart 1.3, static Workbench disk prompt.
  Source: https://www.amigaroms.com/img/screenshots/kickstart-1-3.gif
  Context: https://www.amigaroms.com/
- kickstart-20.gif: Kickstart 2.0 (37.175), animated disk prompt.
  Source: https://amiga.lychesis.net/assets/Commodore/Kickstart20.gif
- kickstart-31.gif: Kickstart 3.1 (40.068), animated disk prompt.
  Source: https://amiga.lychesis.net/assets/Commodore/Kickstart31.gif
  Context for both animations:
  https://amiga.lychesis.net/specials/Animated.html

Historical artwork attribution: Commodore / Commodore-Amiga and the respective
Amiga artwork rightsholders. Downloaded 2026-10-29.

Playback uses the GIF frame timing, with zero/very short delays treated as 100 ms
as in conventional browser GIF playback. Assets are decoded once per process,
not downloaded or decoded during each animation frame.
